AI processing

Reading three consecutive vitals entries

Clinical summary

Patient trend indicates improving monitored parameters. Continue observation and review according to the treating physician's plan.

CareBridge AI's summary is an assistive tool for reviewing trends. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment — all clinical decisions remain with the treating physician.
